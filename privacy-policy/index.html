    Privacy Policy – Kitchen & Bath Trend

    Last Updated: October 2026

    At Kitchen & Bath Trend, accessible through our website, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have when visiting our website.

    By using Kitchen & Bath Trend, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

    1. Information We Collect

    We may collect certain information when you visit or interact with our website, including:

    • Personal Information: Information you voluntarily provide, such as your name or email address when contacting us or subscribing to updates.

    • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring websites.

    • Technical Information: Details such as your browser type, device information, IP address, and operating system, which may be collected automatically.

    You can browse many parts of our website without providing personal information.

    2. How We Use Your Information

    Kitchen & Bath Trend may use collected information to:

    • Operate, maintain, and improve our website.

    • Provide relevant kitchen and bathroom design ideas, articles, and resources.

    • Respond to questions, feedback, and inquiries.

    • Understand visitor preferences and website performance.

    • Send newsletters or updates when you have subscribed.

    • Detect suspicious activity and help protect the security of our website.

    • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

    We do not intend to collect more personal information than is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

    3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

    Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember preferences and understand how visitors use their pages.

    We may use cookies to:

    • Remember website preferences.

    • Analyze website traffic and performance.

    • Understand which content visitors find useful.

    • Support advertising and measure advertising effectiveness, where applicable.

    You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

    4. Third-Party Services

    Kitchen & Bath Trend may use third-party services to support website analytics, advertising, embedded content, or other functionality. These providers may collect information through cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

    We may also link to external websites, including home improvement resources, product websites, and other design-related platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

    5. Advertising and Affiliate Links

    Our website may display advertisements from third-party advertising partners. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide relevant advertisements, depending on your settings and applicable law.

    Kitchen & Bath Trend may also participate in affiliate marketing programs. This means we may earn a commission if you click certain links and make a qualifying purchase, at no additional cost to you.

    Any advertising or affiliate relationships will not change our commitment to providing useful and relevant content for our readers.

    6. How We Protect Your Information

    We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

    However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    7. Sharing of Information

    We do not sell your personal information as a general business practice. We may share information in limited circumstances, including:

    • With service providers that help us operate and maintain our website.

    • When required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.

    • When necessary to protect our rights, website security, or users.

    • In connection with a business transfer, where permitted by applicable law.

    Where required, we take appropriate steps to ensure that information is handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

    8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

    Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

    • Request access to personal information we hold about you.

    • Request correction or deletion of certain personal information.

    • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

    • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

    • Object to or restrict certain types of data processing.

    • Manage cookie preferences and applicable advertising choices.

    To exercise an applicable privacy right, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website. We may need to verify your request before responding.

    9. Children's Privacy

    Kitchen & Bath Trend is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

    10. Data Retention

    We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including providing requested services, maintaining website operations, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

    The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

    11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, legal obligations, or privacy practices.

    Any updates will be published on this page, along with a revised “Last Updated” date. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.
